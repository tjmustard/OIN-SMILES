OIN-SMILES v0.4.4 → v0.4.15 — accuracy & speed retrospective

OIN-SMILES · release retrospective · v0.4.4 → v0.4.15

Twelve releases, two questions, and two scoreboards that were lying.

Accuracy and speed across every point release since v0.4.4 — including the release where the headline number fell ten points on purpose, the release that built a fix worth eight points and then refused to ship it, and the release that finally shipped the safe half of it and cancelled its own 55 CPU-hour re-measurement as the wrong instrument.

Is it more accurate? Yes — 77.16% (measured by a real sweep) at v0.4.13, the first rise in the window. Real gains landed in v0.4.4–v0.4.6; then the default path changed no answer for five releases while the figure fell from 82.80% to 72.46% at v0.4.8 — the measurement was corrected, not the software regressed. v0.4.13 shipped the safe half of a fix that had been built, refuted and shelved two releases earlier, once a veto existed that could prove the harmful half gone on two independent draws.
Is it faster? On the molecules that hit the fixed costs, yes. Corpus-wide, unknown. Three default-path optimisations landed. But no generator sweep has run since v0.4.6, so the corpus figure is stale — and the largest measured single win (81.89 s → 10.87 s) ships behind a lever that is off. v0.4.13 declined to run a sweep on purpose: re-running a stochastic generator to A/B an encoder-side change adds noise to the number it is trying to read.

01

Explain it like I'm five

Think of the project as a machine that turns a 3D molecule into a short piece of text, and back again. It works if the text you get out the second time is exactly the text you put in.

For two releases the machine genuinely got better. Then someone checked the scoreboard and found it was cheating: when the machine was asked "did you rebuild the molecule right?", it had been grading its own homework — looking at what it meant to build instead of what it actually built. Once that was fixed, the score dropped from 83 out of 100 to 72 out of 100.

The machine didn't get worse. The scoreboard got honest.

Then five releases in a row changed nothing about how the machine works. That sounds bad, but it's the opposite of sloppiness. Each one built the thing it was supposed to build, measured it properly, and in most cases discovered the idea was wrong and shelved it. One built a fix worth eight points and then found it worked by throwing away information — quietly turning left-handed molecules into right-handed ones. It was switched off.

The sixth one finally moved the score up: 72 out of 100 to 76. It did that by taking the switched-off fix and adding the safety check that tells its good half from its harmful half — then proving on two separate samples that the harmful half was gone. It also cancelled the expensive re-measurement it was supposed to run, because re-running a machine that makes slightly different choices each time would have added noise to the very number it was trying to read, and a cheaper check gave an exact answer.

Explain it like I'm twenty

Two separate questions, two separate answers.

The v0.4.8 drop needs explaining, and it is not a regression. The harness had been scoring a round trip with get_oin_string(gen_result.mol, coords) — the generator's own bond graph. That is circular: gen_result.mol is precisely the artifact that would have to be wrong for the test to fail. FIYHUT_comp_0 ships both cyclopentadienyl rings 0.85 Å off the iron — ten bonded carbons down to zero — and scored a byte-exact pass.

Re-deriving the verdict from the generated coordinates alone moved 613 molecules down and 30 up. 72.46% and 82.80% describe the same underlying performance; only one of them is true.

The v0.4.13 rise deserves the same scrutiny, because this project has already raised byte_exact once by deleting information. The donor fold moves 393 molecules in one direction with the comparison key untouched — and collapses enantiomers in 221 of them, which neither the metric nor the key can see. What makes +3.42 trustworthy is not the transition matrix. It is that the parity veto zeroes the collapse on two independent draws (19 → 0 cat-only, 33 → 0 mixed) while the achiral population stays unmoved in both, and that every collapsing molecule is accounted for as a separated pair rather than a decline. 171 survivors was also reached from the opposite direction: v0.4.11 bounded the safe set from above at "at most ~172" by counting damage.

No longer stale — measured for this refresh The v0.4.6-era headline (994/5000 = 19.88% over 30 s, median 7.19 s) stood unmeasured for eight releases. The sweep run for this refresh reads 678/5000 = 13.56% and a median of 4.01 s — a 6.3-point improvement no release claimed. Every per-release speed number remains a per-molecule A/B, and every one of those is bimodal: the same change measures −32.9% on one molecule and nothing on the next. ⚠ Not strictly like-for-like — the sweep capped BLAS threads and the v0.4.6 run did not. Goal B (max(elapsed_s) < 30 s) is not delivered: max is 728.8 s.

What actually improved most

Neither number. What improved most is the project's ability to tell a real result from a fake one. The unit suite went 551 → 1007, and the instruments added in that window — the honest re-score, the corpus encoder-identity gate, the two-arm byte-identity gate, the coordination-integrity check, the mirror audit — are what caught the 10.34-point inflation, a 59%-false-positive cohort, a dead gate arm, and an enantiomer collapse. Five releases that moved no points each removed a way of being wrong — and v0.4.13 is the return on that, banking a fix that could only ship once an instrument existed that could see its damage.

04

How to read these numbers

Three traps, all of which this project has already fallen into once and documented.

Trap 1 — the bucket reports are not a time series They come from five different cohorts: 6719 (v0.4.2 capstone), 3917 (v0.4.4 regression, deliberately adversarial), 936 (v0.4.5 re-baseline), 5000 (seed-42 sweep). Every accuracy figure here carries its N and its cohort. Do not draw a line between two of them. The only genuine like-for-like comparison in this document is scored-vs-honest at v0.4.8, which classifies the same 5000 reports two ways.
Trap 2 — metrics.elapsed_s is nested, and it is a sum Read from the top level it silently yields 0. It also accumulates up to three separately SIGKILLed harness attempts, so the retired headline "max 759.9 s against a 300 s budget" was arithmetic on a sum — all 4658 single-attempt rows in the 5k sweep finish within 0.2 s of their cap. The harness enforces to ε ≈ 0.2 s.
Trap 3 — suite count is a rigour proxy, not accuracy 551 → 1007 tests means the project can detect more kinds of wrongness. It does not mean the notation got better.

Release by release

Each card: what shipped · accuracy · speed · what was refuted. Refutations get equal weight to wins — that is what most of these releases produced.

v0.4.4

Accuracy, and the instrument to measure it

Accuracy gain Speed gain

Six parallel worktree swimlanes. The centrepiece is a fac/mer-aware canonical round-trip key that separates a genuine fac↔mer miss from benign slot relabeling, plus the bucket report that decomposes every round trip into six classes.

Accuracy
Real gain. OIN_EARLY_EXIT — accept the first conformer that independently re-encodes to the key, instead of exhausting the pool — took byte-exact 44.7% → 60.5% and key-match 55.3% → 73.7% with zero regressions. A separate 3917-molecule regression sweep measured 11 regressions / 1092 fixes, and all 11 regressions are 300 s timeouts: zero correctness regressions.
Speed
Real gain. OIN_EARLY_EXIT is ~5× faster on the worst cohort. Encoder-side, the AC2BO valence-combination sort was capped and resonance enumeration moved into a forked, CPU-bounded child, recovering previously-unencodable molecules without changing any encodable OIN.
Refuted
Rigid η-winding construction, Kabsch greedy placement, and a stretched-bond acceptance metric all measured net-negative or neutral and kept OFF — a third independent confirmation that selection beats construction in this pipeline.
Caution
The byte_exact 81.19% figure often associated with v0.4.4 is the v0.4.4 key applied to v0.4.2-generated data. It measures the new classifier, not new generation.

v0.4.5

The emitted string becomes canonical

Accuracy gain Speed gain

Six canonicality levers promoted to default-ON together, via a new single-source registry. What made them safe as a block: each repairs a renumbered presentation without rewriting the canonical answer — which is why the corpus shows no churn.

Accuracy
Real gain — and the important number is not byte_exact. On a generator-free probe holding the graph fixed and varying only rotation and atom renumbering (so the correct answer is byte-identical by construction): byte-stability 58.1% → 69.6%, comparison-key instability 60 → 16 molecules. The key is the harness's acceptance predicate and the basis of every accuracy number the project reports — it went from unstable on 1 molecule in 5 to 1 in 19. On the 936-molecule re-baseline, 145 of 436 previously-failing molecules were fixed.
Speed
Real gain, unconditional. The confirming step of OIN_EARLY_EXIT — a full re-perception measured at 48–57 s per call — was running twice per conformer, once during pool fill and again, redundantly, in the selection re-scan. Anything surviving to selection is provably already a confirmed non-match, so the second test recomputed a known answer.
Found
The project's first ever encoder profile: perception_core.AC2BO is 99.8% of a slow encode — not "dominant", essentially all of it. Neither an eta nor a size phenomenon: a 39-atom non-eta molecule profiles the same and is slower.
Refuted
The inverted CIP goldens for two chiral fixtures had been wrong for four months. The test that "verified" them ran rdCIPLabeler on a SMILES reparsed from the encoder's own output — and that call converts a parity tag into a label without checking it, so an inverted tag was self-consistent and passed. Separately, 13% of molecules were found to emit different absolute stereochemistry under pure atom renumbering; no prior instrument could see it.

v0.4.6

Boron, Δ/Λ, and the first crack in the metric

Accuracy gain Speed cost

OIN_BORON_CAGE promoted; a complete metal-centred Δ/Λ descriptor pipeline behind an opt-in lever; and — decisively for everything that follows — OIN_INDEP_SCORE added, recording the honest verdict beside the scored one.

Accuracy
Real, small, correctly priced. 34 of the 36 encode-failing molecules on the re-baseline are electron-deficient boron clusters; the lever takes that population from 0/36 to 34/36. It also moves 14 molecules from scored-passing to failing — which is correct: they passed while describing the wrong graph. It trades 14 silent false positives for 14 loud honest failures, so a headline pass rate can move either way.
Speed
A measured regression, accepted. Promoting the lever moved most of that class from failing instantly to failing slowly: of 33 measured, only 2 generate, 25 burn the entire cap, 6 fail instantly — roughly 2.1 CPU-h wasted per 5000-molecule sweep. One molecule is the two-point proof: lever OFF it fails in 0.87 s; lever ON it encodes a correct B₁₀ cage and then runs past 340 s.
Refuted
Eight hypotheses, each with the measurement that killed it — including "the accuracy gap is mostly compute" (75% of timeouts hide real failures, not latent passes) and three successive Δ/Λ formulations.
Set up v0.4.8
The seed-42 5000-molecule sweep landed here: byte_exact 82.80%, median 7.2 s, 994 molecules over 30 s. Alongside it, the false-positive measurement: 9.6% overall, 28.1% of haptic molecules — net ~5.7 points over-stated. And a lever that "recovered" 90 of 340 failures was held OFF because a coordination check showed those 90 to be 60 degraded / 21 boundary / 9 intact — a claimed +90 is really +9.

v0.4.7

A release of measured negatives

No default change

Five swimlanes. Four refuted the thing they were built to ship, and the fifth refuted its own sibling. Every lever default-OFF.

Accuracy
Zero — and the evidence that it is zero is the deliverable. The sweep's bucket report is byte-for-byte unchanged: byte_exact 4140 / 82.80%, > 30 s 994 / 19.88%.
Refuted
OIN_ACCEPT_SCORED: do not promote, superseding this lever's own earlier "promote" reading. The gate that recommended it was circular — passed is computed with the same predicate the lever accepts on, so "18/22 both arms, zero regressions" could not detect what dropping the strict step costs. With a genuinely independent arm: 15/20 → 7/20, 8 regressions, 0 fixes, one-way. Six of the eight lose haptic coordination outright. Byte-identical notation, changed geometry — the cost is invisible to the very metric that would police it.
Refuted
A boron fast-fail discriminator, caught before it could do harm. One molecule the lane classified as a hopeless cap-burner does generate — in 61.8 s, against a lane sweep capped at 30 s. The class boundary moves with the compute you give it. Cross-tabulated, every geometry with a success also has failures, so geometry separates nothing — and it was the last discriminator standing after hapticity, size and denticity.
Refuted
The encode floor is three cost regimes, not one. A 20-minute encode turned out to be three forked resonance timeouts at 95.8% of the fork budget each — the CPU-limit backstop firing, not slow arithmetic. Tuning the solver would never have touched it.
Built for later
OIN_ATTACH_CHECK (7 of 8 known regressions separated, 0 false positives, 7–81 ms against the 48–57 s test it replaces) — and it never reads a bond object as evidence of attachment, because a detached ligand keeps its bond. Plus the two-arm byte-identity gate, the first instrument that could tell a notation change from a compute change.

v0.4.8

The honest number

Re-baselined −10.34 pts

OIN_INDEP_SCORE promoted to default-ON. No encoder, generator or notation behaviour changed. The measurement changed.

Accuracy
The reported figure falls 10.34 points, on purpose. byte_exact 4140 (82.80%) → 3623 (72.46%); structural 9 → 417. 643 molecules moved bucket: 613 degraded, 30 improved. At corpus scale 36.7% of haptic byte-exact passes were false, against 6.7% non-haptic.
Speed
Unchanged, and stated as such. Nothing was re-generated, so elapsed_s is untouched and > 30 s stays 994 / 19.88%.
Refuted
The recorded suspicion that a ~19× structural inflation on re-encode was an artifact of the method. It is real, and two thirds of it is independently confirmed. Also refuted: the cost argument that had held the lever off. It priced the second encode at "0.4–1.5 s/molecule"; the measured figure is 0.33 s/molecule, and the whole corpus re-scores in 334 s against the ~55 CPU-h a live re-sweep costs. There was never a cost case for scoring dishonestly.
Also found
The atom-count gate is load-bearing, not a third error direction. Of 18 molecules at corpus scale, hydrogen-only in 18/18, 8 re-encode byte-identically to their input — so no string comparison of any kind can separate a structure carrying two extra hydrogens from the original. One molecule defeats four instruments at once: scored PASS, honest PASS (the same string), key EQUAL, coordination INTACT, atom count 61 ≠ 63.
Scope
Deliberately narrow: this changed what is reported, not what is accepted. Scoring the acceptance ladder honestly would move runtime and the failure mix in the same release that re-baselines the number, making both unmeasurable.

v0.4.9

Speed becomes measurable — and the release refutes its own charter

No default change

OIN_ENFORCE_BUDGET (default OFF), a typed budget-exhaustion error, and a frozen stratified 328-molecule runtime benchmark reproducing to 0.28% — the noise floor every later runtime claim must clear.

Accuracy
Zero. Confirmed by a live 300-molecule run: 296/300 per-molecule agreement with the offline re-score, 0/300 encoder drift.
Speed
Nothing promoted — but the goal became expressible for the first time. OIN3DGenerator(timeout=) confirmed advisory: 60 s requested, 60.0–172.8 s spent, worst case 2.9×, because the deadline is checked only at the top of the embed attempt loop and an in-flight attempt always runs to completion.
Refuted
Its own chartered justification. The charter rested on one number — "759.9 s against a 300 s budget" — which is arithmetic on a sum. All 4658 single-attempt rows finish within 0.2 s of their cap. The advisory-timeout defect is real and two direct probes measure it; the corpus number was simply never evidence for it.
Refuted
The first design of its own lever. Threading the deadline into the one function two profiles had indicted measured ε = +48.4 s on a 30 s budget — it changed almost nothing. There is no single sink: three molecules, three dominant costs (75% embed / 77% embed+eigendecomposition / 99% CIP re-parse). A bound threaded into whichever function profiled expensive last is not a bound.
Refuted
The inherited slow cohort is 59% false positive. It selected on the scored verdict — and a slow cohort is made of exactly the haptic molecules the scored verdict over-states most.
Measured, not delivered
With the bound on, ε = +32.8 s on a 30 s budget and the same 11 molecules exceed it in every arm. The bound compresses the tail (2.63× → 2.09×); it does not remove it.
Reordered the roadmap
structural was 9 molecules / 0.18 points when scored dishonestly. Honestly it is 417 / 8.34 points — the second-largest block in the gap.

v0.4.10

Cost per attempt — and the arbiter was broken first

Accuracy flat Speed gain

Two performance changes and a gate repair. The first thing this release measured was its own gate, and the gate was dead.

The gate
ARM 1 had been exiting before comparing anything for a whole release cycle: a fixture was added and the golden never extended, so a hardcoded count guard hard-refused every run. v0.4.9 froze a 328-molecule benchmark and measured a 0.28% noise floor while the encoder arm of its own gate was non-runnable. Behind the refusal sat a second, real drift — a rename that was behaviour-neutral but not string-neutral, and ARM 1 hashes error strings on purpose. The other 60 rows were byte-identical, so the encoder itself had not moved.
Accuracy
Zero, by construction. ARM 1 62/62 and ARM 2 90/90 byte-identical, for both lanes, on all four gate runs.
Speed
One default-path win, one shelved. get_embedding's outer loop called list.index() on a candidate list and threw the result away — not a no-op, because that comparison eigendecomposes a Coulomb matrix per candidate per iteration. Deleted: −32.9% on the molecule that hits it, +0.3% (nil) one molecule over. Ships on by default with no lever — gating provably dead code would permanently ship if not lever: <discarded computation>.
Attributed
The bimodality is explained, not excused as noise: one molecule makes 99 such comparisons costing 38.52 s (38.8% of generation); the other makes 3, costing 0.03 s. Two independent measurements agree on size — 38.8% attribution vs 32.9% quiet A/B.
Corrected
A first A/B taken while four gate processes competed for the box (load average 35) reported −50.2% and +9.6%. Quiet-box re-measurement gives −32.9% and +0.3% — the gain was over-stated by 17 points and the null was buried in 30% within-arm spread. Byte-identity gates are load-immune and can be parallelised freely; wall-clock is neither.
Refuted
The chartered Lane 1's premise does not reproduce — all 165 calls converge in exactly one iteration against a cap of 30, and the charter's "1189 calls / 15.6 s" measures 74 calls / 1.75 s. Chartered Lane 2 was already fixed by an earlier release. Executing the charter as written would have spent the release on its third-best target.
Not done, deliberately
The CIP memo — 81.89 s → 10.87 s, −86.7%, the largest measured single speed win in the project's history — is not promoted. The charter permits same-release promotion only if byte-identity holds on the whole benchmark; this release ran the fast band, 90 of 328. Fast-band-only evidence is not that. So it ships off.

v0.4.11

The fix that worked, and had to be refused

No default change

The lane built the fix v0.4.5 had specified in writing for the largest single block in the gap — 496 molecules, 9.92 points. It works exactly as designed. It also destroys information.

It works
393 molecules move slot_renumber → byte_exact, none in any other direction. facmer_divergent holds at 16, the comparison key moves on 0 of 992 strings, both gate arms stay byte-identical with the lever off. +7.86 points.
And it collapses enantiomers
A mirror audit on a uniform 250-molecule draw found 19 (7.6%) whose mirror image encodes identically once the fold is on. Run directly on the 393 molecules the fold claims as wins: 221 of 393 (56.2%) collapse. An independent geometric oracle sharing no machinery with the encoder confirms 18 of 19 uniform-draw collapses. More than half the gain is the damage.
Why the safety argument failed
It assumed two donors in one symmetry class are interchangeable. The symmetry computed is that of the isolated ligand graph, but those donors sit at distinct vertices whose relation to the other ligands is chirality-bearing — so the vertex permutation their exchange induces can be improper. A fragment's automorphism says nothing about the parity of the vertex permutation it induces. v0.4.5's restriction to proper rotations was not conservatism; it was the load-bearing correctness condition.
The finding that outranks the points byte_exact can be raised by deleting information, and the comparison key will agree. Both are blind to reflection, because the key folds that axis deliberately. A one-directional transition matrix is not evidence of safety for anything touching canonicalization. Two independent cohorts find the damage (uniform 19/250, stratified 31/300), and the named Δ/Λ fixtures both pass with the lever on — fixtures alone could never have caught this.

v0.4.12

The parity filter

No default change

Released on main at 675e5425, pyproject 0.4.12 — no v0.4.12 tag yet. The lane built the reflection-parity filter v0.4.11's refutation demanded, and it works.

Accuracy
Flat, deliberately — both levers ship OFF. But the filter works: a uniform 250-molecule mirror audit goes 19 → 0 collapses, with those 19 moving into "distinct in both arms" (73 → 92) and the achiral/pre-existing-fold class unmoved at 157 — direct evidence that the veto did not simply refuse to fold everything. The surviving gain is +3.42 points (171 of 393). v0.4.11 bounded the safe set at "at most ~172" by counting collapses; this filter counts survivors through the shipped predicate and lands on 171. Two independent routes agreeing to one molecule.
Two seeds
The gate holds at a second, disjoint draw — which is the part that matters. v0.4.11's recorded methodology lesson was that one draw cannot separate a corpus property from a sampling artifact. Seed 11 answers it: the baseline independently reproduces 19/250 on a disjoint sample, so the 7.6% collapse rate is a property of the corpus, not of seed 7. The veto zeroes both seeds while the achiral population stays untouched at 157 and 141 — so it separates the right set rather than declining to fold. That the achiral count is unmoved is the second-most-important number here: a veto without that left conjunct would have swallowed all 157 as well, still reported zero regressions, and looked like a total success while destroying the fold's entire benefit.
Speed
No runtime claim in the release. The eta lever's timing A/B was stopped rather than banked at load 26 — never interleave timing runs with gate runs — so its predicted tail reduction shipped unverified.

⚠ Post-release, not part of v0.4.12. A lane commit on swimlane/v0412-eta-accept (unmerged at the time of writing) re-ran that A/B on a quiet box against a re-derived 12-molecule population: median 61.8 s → 18.82 s (−69.5%), total −47.4%, >30 s 11 → 5, with the same bimodal signature as v0.4.10 — one molecule 119.04 s → 4.4 s, a 27× win with a byte-identical string; two molecules nil. The runtime thesis is confirmed — and the lever still stays off. A fifth gate arm, the metal-configuration check v0.4.11 paid for, caught one molecule returning a different Δ/Λ descriptor with a byte-identical emitted string; four of the five gates call that molecule untouched, and any Δ/Λ divergence is blocking. A large real speedup that costs something four of five instruments cannot see is precisely the trade this release exists to detect.
Refuted before it was built
Reflection parity is not a property of the emitted string: a donor swap is a transposition fixing every other vertex, so the obvious determinant test on the polyhedron rejects every swap and degenerates the fold to the identity. The veto had to run on the pristine conformer instead.
Two silent defects
Both caught only by disbelieving a clean result. The molecule's atom order is not the coordinate order — zipping positionally invented bonds outright. And the mirror was encoded with the fold inherited, which disarmed the achiral guard and made the self-check decline on 18 of 18 movers while all three fixture tests passed — because declining to fold also separates a mirror pair.
The transferable lesson Ask what a broken version of the instrument would print. If it is the same thing, you have measured nothing.

v0.4.13

The fold ships — and the release cancels its own 55 CPU-hour precondition

Accuracy gain

Released as tag v0.4.13, main at 9fbdf678. The first upward move in the releases this report covers — banked by shipping a fix built, measured, refuted and shelved two releases earlier. v0.4.14 later corrected the size of it downward, because the instrument that measured it could not report a loss.

Accuracy
Real gain, but smaller than published. Published +3.42 (77.16% (measured by a real sweep)); measured ~+2.42 (→ ~74.88%). 171 molecules were reported moving key_equal/slot_renumber → byte_exact with 0 in a bad direction. The 0 is the artifact — it came from an offline re-score, which holds the generated structure fixed and so cannot express a loss. Measured end-to-end by v0.4.14: gains 22/25 = 88% ⇒ ~150 real, losses 6/40 = 15% over a 197-molecule at-risk population nobody had sampled ⇒ ~30 losses. OIN_CANONICAL_DONOR_FOLD and OIN_FOLD_PARITY_VETO are promoted to default-ON together — one promotion with two names, pinned by a test so neither can be demoted alone, because the fold without the veto is the configuration v0.4.11 refuted and no gate, golden or comparison key can see that damage.
The gate, twice
Run on two independent draws, and the accounting is what carries it: cat-only goes 19 → 0 collapses (achiral unmoved at 157, 73 + 19 = 92) and the mixed cat+photo draw goes 33 → 0 (achiral unmoved at 134, 83 + 33 = 116). A veto that works and a veto that declines everything both report zero — v0.4.12's own first implementation declined on 18 of 18 while every fixture test passed. Here every collapsing molecule lands in the separated class and nothing else moves, so the zero is bought by separating, not abstaining.
Refuted: its own sweep
The chartered ~55 CPU-hour re-sweep was not run, and running it would have been worse evidence. The 72.46% baseline is itself an offline re-score, not a sweep — no generator sweep has run since v0.4.6. Re-running a stochastic generator to A/B an encoder-side change contaminates the signal with run-to-run variation and is not even like-for-like with the table it is compared against. What licensed the offline route was measured: acceptance decides by comparison key, and across 9669 corpus strings the fold moves 1019 and changes 0 keys. The 1019 is the load-bearing half — a lever that never fired would also print zero.
Two n = 1 classes, finally sized
Over the 767 genuine failures: 280 DETACHED (the generator returned a structure with ligands off the metal) and 99 INTACT (attachment fine, re-perception still disagrees), against a control of 1.32% detached among passing molecules versus 24.11% among failing — an 18.2× enrichment. v0.4.14

The release that corrected its own instrument

Accuracy gain

Released as tag v0.4.14. It promoted one lever and gained +1.42 points — then found that the method both it and v0.4.13 had used to measure themselves could not report a loss at all, and corrected both headlines downward.

Accuracy
Real gain — +1.42 points, 78 gains against 7 losses, net +71 molecules, measured end-to-end over all 182 molecules the lever can affect (n = 182 of 182, nothing sampled). OIN_RESONANCE_DONOR_FOLD ships default-ON: it widens the donor fold's equivalence test to the fragment's constitutional skeleton — bond orders, aromatic flags, charges and hydrogens erased; connectivity, element and chiral tag kept — so acac written ketone/enol, carboxylates and sulfonates stop reading as two inequivalent donors. Ester -O-/=O, ether/ketone and amide N/O still do not merge. The release first reported +1.56 with bad_direction = 0; correcting that is its real content.
Refuted
Its own measurement method, and the previous release's. The licence for measuring offline was fold_key_invariance.py reading 0 comparison keys changed. That bounds acceptance — accept_fn decides by key — but the generator's input is the OIN string, so a slot relabeling changes the parsed structure, the coordinate map, and the pool itself. And an offline re-score holds the generated structure fixed, so for a molecule that already round-trips it can only print “still fine”. The population nobody had sampled is the one that can only lose: movers that were already passing — 39 here, 197 for v0.4.13.
Two bucket names that were hypotheses
key_equal is documented as “benign canonicalization”; 183 of its 361 members (50.7%) are the generator building the ENANTIOMER, invisible because the comparison key folds reflection deliberately and accept_fn decides by that key (222/222 classified, 0 excluded). And rdkit_canonical is 80.7% η-set denticity drift — the generated ring slips and fewer carbons fall inside the bonding cutoff — not canonicality at all. 5.94 points re-filed off the encoder ladder without a single molecule changing bucket, leaving it about 1.28 points of reachable work.
Also measured (no code change)
PREFILTER_VETO's prevalence, which v0.4.13 could only size at n = 1: over a stratified 50-molecule sample the cheap prefilter vetoed 261 conformers and the strict test disagreed on 4 — 1.5% of vetoes, 4.5% of molecules. It stays off, but its latency objection is refuted: it is measured faster. Anyone citing cost to keep it off is citing a refuted number.
What it cost
7 molecules that round-tripped no longer do. They are not the encoder emitting a wrong string — it is canonical in both arms. The generator, handed an equally valid but differently-labelled input, builds a worse structure. Every future canonicalization lever pays this toll.
The transferable lesson An offline re-score reports bad_direction = 0 whether or not losses exist. Ask what a broken version of an instrument would print — here, the same thing a working one does.

Zero, by design

Accuracy

Lane 2 replaced its charter outright. Believed for an hour.

Refresh the report at any release close-out. The command re-derives the figures from the sources, appends one release section, updates the verdicts and the gap table, and republishes this page to the same URL. 

/release-retrospective v0.4.13      # append one release (the common case)

It lives at .claude/commands/release-retrospective.md, and it carries the standing caveats with it — cohorts are not comparable, elapsed_s is nested and a sum, the corpus speed figure is stale until a sweep says otherwise, and a lever measured but shipped default-OFF changes nothing for a user.
