- `NOTES.md` is the session-persistent source of truth; read it before starting and append a log entry after.

## Patterns & Lessons

- **Driver-side independent verification pays for itself.** Re-deriving the brief's original failing measurement (not just re-running the executor's green tests) confirmed real correctness and caught executor-introduced lint/docstring issues.
- **Match by content, not position, when diagnosing canonicalization.** The haptic-face "mismatch" only became legible once the two rings were aligned by substituent content; that revealed it as a winding-preserving relabeling, not a reflection bug.
- **Guard against tooling that silently edits.** `ruff --fix` deleting docstring text is a data-loss failure mode; always re-read after an autofix before committing.
- **Adapt skills to actual state.** `/hyper-publish` assumes uncommitted changes; with a clean tree its real job was pushing already-committed work — the outward-facing push still went through an explicit approval gate.
- **Keep the outward-facing gate even when invoked to publish.** Invoking a publish skill authorizes intent, but the specific landing (direct-to-main vs. PR) and the tag were still surfaced for explicit choice.
