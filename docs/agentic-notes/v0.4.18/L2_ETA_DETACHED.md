## 1. The number — `tools/v0418/eta_distance_audit.py`

No atom mapping between the two structures, deliberately. Ligands are the connected components of the
non-metal covalent graph, matched input ↔ generated by formula; for `k` input contact atoms of
element `e`, the generated side contributes its `k` shortest M–`e` distances inside the matched
ligand. `inflation = mean_gen − mean_in`. Contact cutoff = the encoder's own, `S + 0.45 Å`
(`S` = covalent-radius sum).

**Control first:** the input against itself prints inflation **0.000 Å**, ≥ 99.0% in band, 0 unmatched
ligands over 5,000.

On the v0.4.17 sweep's 4,780 structures (408 unmatched ligands in 236 molecules — counted, not
dropped):

| contact groups | class | n | median inflation | p10 … p90 | atoms still in band |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| **η** | verified pass | 838 | **+0.170 Å** | −0.38 … +0.39 | 94.5% |
| **η** | **DETACHED** | 434 | **+0.728 Å** | −0.06 … +1.11 | **18.4%** |
| η | other fail | 272 | +0.312 Å | | 55.8% |
| σ | verified pass | 12,604 | **−0.313 Å** | −0.47 … −0.01 | 99.8% |
| σ | DETACHED | 848 | +0.221 Å | | 69.8% |

By metal (η, median inflation; detached vs verified molecules in the census): **Fe +0.835 (45 vs 4) ·
Co +0.775 (21 vs 2) · Ni +0.714 (39 vs 11)** · Ru +0.37 · Ir +0.28 · Rh +0.23 · Zr/Ti +0.18. The
generated M–C(η) distance barely depends on the metal; the real one does. Small metals lose.

## 2. Mechanism 1 — the target is aimed at the edge of the bonding range

`ff_clean` sets each binding group's target distance, then multiplies an η group's by upstream's

```python
self.scale_factor = {1: 1, 2: 1.1, 3: 1.1, 4: 1.1, 5: 1.2, …, 9: 1.2, "else": 1.6}
#  "Consider elongation of haptic interaction ..."
```

Measured over **1,378 η groups** of the cohort, real M–C(η) = **1.008 × S**. The encoder calls an atom
bonded inside `S + 0.45 Å` ≈ **1.20 × S**. An η⁵ ring is aimed *at* the cutoff — and beyond it for
every metal with `S > 2.25 Å`. `OIN_ETA_COVALENT_TARGET` drops the factor.

## 3. Mechanism 2 — a dead zone in the clash guard

The FF scan walks each binding group toward its target in 0.1 Å steps and **reverts a step that adds
a vdW clash** (`clash.VDW_ACCEPTANCE_ENABLED`, default ON since v0.4.3 A5). `vdw_clash_count`
perceives bonds by distance: bonded inside `1.3 × ΣR_cov`, a clash if non-bonded inside
`0.75 × ΣR_vdW`. For a small metal the first threshold lies **inside** the second:

| M–C | bond below | clash below | dead zone |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Fe | 2.70 Å | 2.81 Å | **0.11 Å** |
| Co | 2.63 | 2.78 | 0.15 |
| Ni | 2.60 | 2.78 | 0.18 |
| Zn | | | 0.28 |
| Au · Cu · Pt | | | 0.09 · 0.07 · 0.06 |
| Ru, Rh, Ir, Ti, Zr, Mo, W | | | none |

The dummy-atom embed leaves a Cp ring at ~2.87 Å. Its first step inward puts five carbons in the dead
zone: five new "clashes", the step is reverted, **the scan stops, and the ring stays at 2.87 Å
whatever the target.** That is why Mechanism 1 alone gains nothing on Fe/Co/Ni, and why those three
metals are 105 detached against 17 verified.

`OIN_VDW_EXEMPT_BINDING` tells the guard that the metal's own binding atoms are bonded — which they
are, by construction. **Scope, measured:** exempting the η atoms alone kept 20 of the 22 probe gains;
in six Fe/Co/Ni half-sandwiches a σ donor (a CO carbon) has to cross the zone too. So every binding
atom is exempt, **but only in a complex that has an η group** — a non-η molecule never reaches either
lever and is byte-identical by construction (checked: 25 of 25 built non-η structures on dead-zone
metals byte-identical; the other 7 of 32 errored identically in both arms).

## 5e. `/refreeze-goldens` — 0 of 425 rows, and the zero is a finding about the GATE

The full ARM 2 gate was run (every row of both goldens, shipped defaults, 6 shards), per the owner's
rule. **Every compared row reproduced: 100 of 100 and 272 of 272** (+42 SHA1-only, 5 deterministic

| check | result |
|---|---|
| field 2, all 425 rows, encode-only, `--generator-side` | 421 SAME + 4 sentinels · moved 0 · **stale 0** — the levers do not reach the encoder, and the v0.4.17 full re-freeze holds |

**The levers fired in the gate, and the gate cannot see it.** `tools/gate_arm2_roundtrip_one.py`
computes `smiles_2 = get_oin_string(result.mol, coords)` — through the **generator's own bond graph**.
Whether a ring sits 2.1 Å or 2.9 Å from its metal never enters; the metal–ring bonds are read off
`result.mol`. That is the pre-v0.4.8 *scored* path (false-pass rate 9.6%, 28.1% on haptics). On
`FEPZIK_comp_0` field 3 is identical with the levers on and at `"0"` while the honest round trip goes
FAIL → PASS. The harness's honest score moved on +197 / −28 molecules for this same change.

So **ARM 2 gates the encoder and the generator's graph + stereo, not what the generator BUILDS.** A
construction regression — the exact class of defect this lane repaired — would pass it, and it passed
it for every release that shipped ferrocene detached. The goldens carry a v0.4.18 block saying so
(0 data rows changed; manifests unchanged: `59fe3710…`, `be0c2398…`).
